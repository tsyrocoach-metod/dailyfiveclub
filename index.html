FAQ

Questions

Is this a subscription?

No. It’s a one-time payment. You download the PDF and it’s yours to keep.

How do I get the guide?

Right after payment you’ll get a download link on screen and by email. Save the PDF to your phone, tablet or computer.

Can I fill it in on my iPad?

Yes. Open the PDF in the free Adobe Acrobat Reader and tap any box to check it or any line to type. Prefer handwriting? Open it in GoodNotes or Notability.

Do I need equipment or a gym?

No. A sturdy chair, a wall, a towel and a tennis ball are all you need.

I’m not in great shape. Is it too hard?

It starts with three gentle moves and grows by one a day. Every strength move has an easier version, and the rule is simple: pain means stop.

Do I have to count calories or weigh myself?

No. The food guide uses your hands to measure portions, and progress is measured by energy, sleep and simple tests. Weighing yourself is optional.

Which guide should I start with?

If you want more energy and less stiffness, start with 5-Minute Moves. If your biggest struggle is food, start with 5-Minute Food Habits. If you want both, the bundle costs less than two guides.

Is this medical advice?

No. If you have a health condition, an injury, diabetes or take medication, talk to your doctor before you start.
